What's New in Mamadera 1.3.1

Improvements
- The language-change confirmation now appears in the new language
- The baby's age in the menu now reads in the app's language and counts by the true anniversary
- Better readability of every home button's label, the values and the unit selector in both light and dark modes
- Removed the reminder pills from the home buttons (the due-reminders list stays below the grid); the 'Last tracked' line now reads in the app's language
- History icons now wear the colour of their event type, matching the home buttons
- The 'Pee & Poop' icon in history now joins the droplet family

Fixes
- Restoring a backup no longer refuses a file where a reminder was ticked on more than one day
- The baby's name now appears in the menu right after first-launch setup, without a restart
