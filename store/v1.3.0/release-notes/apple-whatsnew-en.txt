What's New in Mamadera 1.3.0

New features
- System notifications when a reminder is due, even with the app closed — and off until you turn them on, in Reminders
- Your own notification time, chosen in Reminders and applied to every reminder
- One notification per baby: each message carries the baby's name, so two babies never look like one
- Notification preferences now live inside the backup, so a restored phone keeps them instead of asking again

Improvements
- Still nothing to send: notifications are assembled on the device, and the manual export through the share sheet you open remains the only way your data leaves it
