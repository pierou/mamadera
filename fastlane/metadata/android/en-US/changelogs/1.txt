First stable release of Mamadera, a privacy-first newborn tracking app.
• Baby profiles: create, edit, duplicate, delete (with cascade-aware cleanup)
• Feedings (breast & bottle, with duration), sleep, diapers, health routines
• Health reminders: Vitamin D (daily), Vitamin K (rolling 30 days), cleaning care
• History with filters, inline editing and deletion
• 100% offline: data never leaves the device, sensitive fields encrypted at rest
• French, English and Spanish
