Mamadera is a privacy-first newborn tracking app for parents who want to log their baby's feedings, sleep, diaper changes, and health routines — without any cloud dependency or telemetry.

**Key features:**

* Track breastfeeding and bottle feedings with duration logging & notes
* Log naps and nighttime sleep sessions
* Record wet/dirty diaper changes at a glance
* Health routine reminders (eye/face/nose cleaning, belly button care, Vitamin D/K)
* Multiple baby profiles with active profile selection
* Chronological event history with filtering, editing, and deletion
* Localized in French, English, and Spanish

**Privacy & security:**

* 100% offline — all data stays on your device
* No analytics, no telemetry, no cloud sync
* End-to-end encryption for sensitive notes (AES-GCM)
* Minimal permissions — nothing collected without explicit consent
* Open source under the MIT license
